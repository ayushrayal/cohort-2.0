Dua Lipa is an English singer and songwriter. After signing with Warner Records in 2015, she released her eponymous debut studio album in 2017, which spawned the UK top 10 singles "Be the One", "IDGAF" and the UK number one "New Rules", the latter of which also became her first entry on the US Billboard Hot 100 chart.
